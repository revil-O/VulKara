## Image to image: Fibo 2.0 Edit Turbo

Bria AI's Fibo 2.0 Edit Turbo edits a picture by instruction, repaints a masked part, or combines several pictures. The start picture was made with Z-Image Turbo. Both runtimes with the same seed: ComfyUI (int8 files, float16) for Macs with 20 GB and more, MLX (8 bit) for 16 GB Macs. 1024 × 1024, 4 steps, no guidance, seed 12.
