Model streaming

Big models. Small Mac.

VulKara streams a model from the SSD – internal or external – block by block while the picture is computed. Only the part that is working sits in memory, so models that need 24 GB run on a Mac with 16 GB, at full 16-bit quality.

Internal or external SSD

Keep models and the streaming cache on the Mac’s SSD or on an external drive over USB or Thunderbolt. The faster the drive, the faster the picture.

Full quality

No squeezing into 4 bits: the model keeps its 16-bit weights. As much as fits stays in memory, the rest is read ahead while the GPU works.

Automatic

The memory plan decides for each model: it runs in RAM when it fits, and is streamed from the SSD (internal/external) when it does not. SSD streaming can also be switched off by hand!

Tested on a Mac mini M4 with 16 GB: FLUX.1 [dev], Krea 2 and Qwen-Image 2.1 run. Streaming trades speed for size – big models take minutes per picture.

Next planned features

Your own characters

Create a character once – face, hair, outfit, style – and put them into any scene as a reference: comic panels, anime shots, a whole story. The same character in every picture. Made and kept on your Mac, like everything else.

One character, seven pictures: the reference and six scenes, made with VulKara today (FLUX.2 klein with the reference picture on the Image page). The feature will make it a few clicks.

Pose editor in 3D

Turn a pose freely in space and look at your figure from any angle: from the front, the side, above or a worm’s-eye view. The result goes into Text to image as a pose, as before.

Turn freely

Drag the figure to move the camera around it, up and down. Fixed views with one click: from the front, from the side, from behind, from above.

Joints like a body

Dragging a point turns its joint, and the bones keep their length. Head, shoulders, hips, knees and fingers stay within natural limits – switch them off for acrobatics.

Save your poses

Keep a pose and its viewpoint as a template, load it again, rename it or pass it on as a file.

For your characters

A saved pose goes straight into a character scene, together with its viewpoint.

Features

One app, a whole studio

Pictures, edits, video and your own LoRAs – for creators, students, comic and anime fans and digital artists.

Text to image

Describe it, pick a style, press ⌘↩. Twenty styles from photo to illustration and 3D, and up to three LoRAs, each with its own weight.

Image to image and inpainting

Keep the motif, change the scene. Or paint over just the part that should change – the rest stays untouched.

AI Canvas

An endless surface: move the frame over the edge and Generate continues the picture. Paint a mask to redo a part.

Image to video

Bring a picture to life with Wan 2.2: describe the movement and get 1–5 seconds at 480p or 720p.

Upscale and photo tools

2× or 4× with Real-ESRGAN, plus sharpen, denoise, remove JPEG artefacts, remove the background and select objects with a click.

Restore old photos

Colour casts, dust and scratches fixed, blurred faces restored, black-and-white photos coloured.

Pose editor

OpenPose skeletons with hands and faces, templates, or a pose detected from a photo – then use it in Text to image.

Illusions

Hide a spiral, a pattern, a word or a logo inside a picture.

LoRA training

Teach a model your art style or character from 10–30 of your own pictures, on SDXL, FLUX.2 [klein] or Krea 2, with a live loss curve.

Many models

SD 1.5, SDXL (also Pony, Illustrious, NoobAI), SD 3.5, FLUX.1, FLUX.2, FLUX 3, Chroma, Qwen-Image, Z-Image, Krea 2, Ideogram 4, Anima and Wan 2.2 video. Download straight from a list of tested models, import from Hugging Face or Civitai (an API token may be needed) by link (URL or creator/model name), or from a model file you already downloaded.

Benchmark: measure your Mac New

Pick a model and measure it on your own Mac: seconds per step, the whole picture, the most memory VulKara and the system used, swap, what was read from the SSD, and every phase from loading to generating. Copy the runs as a table and compare Macs, sizes and bit sizes. The same measurements made VulKara faster: SD 1.5 twice as fast with half the memory, large SDXL pictures four times as fast.

Why VulKara

Your ideas stay yours

Everything on your Mac

Pictures, videos, restorations and training run on the Apple GPU. Your prompts and pictures never leave the Mac – the internet is only used to download runtimes, models and LoRA descriptions and to look for updates. Disconnect your Mac from the network and try it – it also works completely offline (once every package it depends on has been downloaded!)

From your phone, on your own network

Open VulKara in a browser on your phone, tablet or another computer, on your home network or over a VPN connection. See the current status of your pictures, type in and start new ideas while you are out, and look through, download or share finished pictures in the history. Only your Mac computes. Access is secured by an access token and encrypted over HTTPS, to keep your privacy here too.

Switch it on once, scan the QR code, done. Nothing goes to the internet.

Free, no strings

No account, no subscription, no credits. The source code is open: read it, change it, share it for any noncommercial use.

Some models ask for a free Hugging Face or Civitai token before they download.

Runs on small Macs too

A model too big for the memory streams from the SSD block by block, at full quality. On a Mac mini M4 with 16 GB, FLUX.1 [dev], Krea 2 and Qwen-Image 2.1 run.

Streaming trades speed for size: big models take minutes per picture. Stable Diffusion 1.5 runs with 8 GB.

Speaks your language

English, Deutsch, Español, Português (Brasil) and Português (Portugal) – the app and the user guide. Yours is missing? A new language is a folder of text files.

Get started

Your first picture in 3 steps

  1. Install

    Download the DMG, open it and drag VulKara into Applications.

  2. Runtime and model

    Settings → Runtimes: install Diffusers (Apple GPU). Then Models → Recommended: SDXL 1.0 with 16 GB, Stable Diffusion 1.5 with 8 GB.

  3. Create

    Open Text, describe your picture and press ⌘↩. The Start page ticks off each step.
